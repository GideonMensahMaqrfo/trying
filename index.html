Common questions

Quick answers about tracking, quotes and customs.

All questions
Where do I find my tracking number?

It is on your booking confirmation and receipt. It starts with CB followed by letters and numbers. Enter it on the tracking page.

How long does shipping take?

It depends on the route and transport. As a guide, air freight takes a few days, road freight within Europe a few days to a week, and ocean freight several weeks. Your quote shows the expected transit time.

Who pays import duties and taxes?

It depends on the Incoterm agreed between buyer and seller. Usually the receiver pays import duties and VAT. See our Incoterms guide.

How do I pay for my shipment?

We send an invoice by email from our company address with our bank details. We never ask for payment by gift card, cryptocurrency or money transfer apps. Read our fraud advice.

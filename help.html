Tracking

Where do I find my tracking number?

Your tracking number is on your booking confirmation, receipt and in the email we send when your shipment is booked. It starts with CB.

Can I track several shipments at once?

Yes. On the tracking page enter up to 10 tracking numbers separated by commas or spaces.

My tracking number shows “not found”. What should I do?

Check the number for typing mistakes (the letter O and the number 0 are easy to mix up). New bookings can take a few hours to appear. If it still does not work, email us with your booking details.

Why hasn’t my tracking changed for a few days?

During ocean crossings and while goods wait at a port or in customs, there may be no new scan for several days. This is normal. We update the status as soon as the shipment reaches its next stage.

Can I share tracking with my customer?

Yes. After tracking a shipment, click Copy tracking link and send that link to anyone who needs it.

Quotes & booking

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the quote form with the route, type of cargo, weight and size. We reply by email with a written quote.

What information do you need for an accurate quote?

Collection and delivery addresses, a description of the goods, the number of pieces, total weight, size of each piece (or total volume), the date the goods are ready and, if you know it, the Incoterm agreed with your buyer or supplier.

How is the price worked out?

Freight is charged on the greater of the actual weight and the volumetric weight. Our volumetric weight calculator shows how this works. The quote also lists collection, handling, documents, customs and delivery charges where they apply.

How long does shipping take?

As a guide: air freight takes a few days, road freight within Europe usually a few days to a week, and ocean freight several weeks depending on the route. Your quote shows the expected transit time for your shipment.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (full container load) means you use a whole container. LCL (less than container load) means your goods share a container with other customers, so you only pay for the space you use.

Can you collect from my supplier?

Yes. We can collect from your supplier’s address, consolidate goods from several suppliers in our warehouse and ship them together.

Customs & duties

Who pays import duties and taxes?

This depends on the Incoterm agreed between buyer and seller. Usually the receiver pays import duty and VAT, unless the shipment is sent DDP (Delivered Duty Paid). See our Incoterms guide.

Which documents do I need?

Most shipments need a commercial invoice, a packing list and a transport document. Some goods need certificates or licences. See the document checklist.

What is an EORI number?

An EORI number identifies businesses that import or export goods to or from the EU or the UK. Businesses usually need one for customs declarations. You can apply for one with the customs authority in your country.

Why is my shipment held in customs?

Common reasons are missing or incorrect documents, unclear goods descriptions, unpaid duties, or a routine inspection. We will contact you if customs need anything from you. Only pay duties to the official customs authority or on an invoice from our company email.

Packing & cargo

How should I pack my goods?

Use strong boxes or crates, fill empty space, put heavy items at the bottom and label every piece. See our packing tips.

Can I ship batteries, perfume or chemicals?

Often yes, but they count as dangerous goods and need special packing and paperwork. Tell us exactly what you are sending. See restricted items.

Do you ship cars and vehicles?

Yes, by container or roll-on/roll-off (RoRo) depending on the route. We need the vehicle’s registration document and the owner’s ID.

Should I insure my cargo?

We recommend it for valuable goods. A carrier’s liability is limited by international conventions and is often much less than the value of the goods. We can arrange cargo insurance with your booking.

Payment & security

How do I pay?

We send an invoice by email from an @continentalbridgefreightline.com address, with our company bank details. Pay only to the account shown on our invoice.

Someone asked me to pay a fee to release my parcel. Is it real?

Be careful. We never ask for payment by gift card, cryptocurrency or money transfer apps, and we never ask you to pay a private person. If you are unsure, do not pay and contact us using the details on this website. Read our fraud advice.

How do you use my personal data?

We use your details only to quote, ship and deliver your goods and to contact you about them. See our privacy policy.

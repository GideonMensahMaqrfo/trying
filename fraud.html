Fraud awareness

Criminals sometimes pretend to be shipping companies to take money or personal details. Here is how to spot them and stay safe.

We will never ask you to pay by gift card, cryptocurrency, Western Union, MoneyGram or a money transfer app, ask you to pay a private person, or ask for your bank PIN or passwords.

Check the sender

Our emails come only from addresses ending in @continentalbridgefreightline.com. Free email accounts (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo) using our name are not us.

Check the website

Our only website is continentalbridgefreightline.com. Look closely for extra words, hyphens or different endings in links you receive.

Unexpected fees

Be suspicious of sudden demands for “clearance”, “insurance” or “release” fees on a parcel you did not book, especially with a short deadline.

Online friends and sellers

A common scam: someone you met online says they have sent you a parcel, then a “shipping company” asks you to pay to release it. Do not pay.

Check tracking yourself

Type your tracking number on our own tracking page instead of clicking links in messages.

Ask us first

Not sure a message is genuine? Do not pay. Contact us using the details on our contact page and we will check it.

If you think you have been targeted

  • Stop all contact and do not send any more money or personal details.
  • If you have paid, contact your bank straight away.
  • Keep the messages, emails and payment receipts.
  • Report it to the police. In the Netherlands you can report fraud to the police and the Fraudehelpdesk; in the UK to Action Fraud (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) or Police Scotland.
  • Forward suspicious emails that use our name to our team so we can warn other customers.

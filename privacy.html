Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Continental Bridge Freight Line (“we”, “us”) provides freight forwarding and logistics services from offices in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at our contact page.

What we collect

  • Quote and contact requests: your name, company, email, phone number, route, cargo details and message.
  • Shipments: sender and receiver names, addresses, contact details, goods descriptions and tracking events.
  • Staff accounts: email address and login information for our own staff.

Our public tracking page shows only the route, status, location and history of a shipment. It does not show names, addresses or contact details.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and prepare quotes (steps before a contract).
  • To transport, clear and deliver your goods (performing our contract).
  • To meet customs, tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation).
  • To keep our website and systems secure (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only those who need it to move your goods or run our services: carriers, ports, airports, customs authorities and agents at origin and destination, and our technology providers, including our database host (Supabase), website host (GitHub Pages) and email provider. Shipments outside the European Economic Area and the UK require us to share data with parties in the destination country; we do this only as far as needed for the shipment.

How long we keep it

Quote requests: up to 2 years. Shipment and invoice records: as long as tax and customs law requires, usually 7 years.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Staff who log in to the admin area have their login session stored in their browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. You can also complain to the data protection authority: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) in the UK.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

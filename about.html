Our story

More than 15 years of moving freight

Continental Bridge Freight Line started trading in 2011 with a simple aim: to give businesses and families one reliable partner for getting goods from one continent to another.

Over the years we have built our service around sea, air and road freight, warehousing and customs clearance, and added online tracking so customers can follow every shipment themselves. Today our head offices in Amsterdam and the United Kingdom coordinate shipments between Europe, Africa and the rest of the world.

Who we are

A bridge between your supplier and your customer

Our name says what we do. We build the link between the place your goods are made and the place they are needed, whether that is across the North Sea or across the world.

From our offices in Amsterdam, close to one of Europe’s largest port and airport hubs, and in the United Kingdom, we plan and manage shipments by sea, air and road. We take care of booking, documents, customs and final delivery, so our customers only need to deal with one company.

Every shipment receives its own tracking number, and our team updates its status at each stage, so you always know where your cargo is.
